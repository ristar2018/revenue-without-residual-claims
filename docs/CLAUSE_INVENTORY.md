## Fee formulas

Seven records contain a fee linked to revenue or profit, covering four formula
types: (i) 8% of hotel revenue plus 5% of operating profit, reported in three
consecutive years for one clause; (ii) a four-step entitlement of 100, 92, 90
and 88% as receipts rise, reported for two parks under one agreement; (iii) a
progressive excess schedule applied above a basic fee; (iv) retention of profit
above a contracted target. The remaining records state a fixed or cost-based
amount, or refer to the notes without a formula.
